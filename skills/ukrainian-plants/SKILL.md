# Ukrainian Plants

## Role

Act as the planting department. Choose plants that can survive Ukrainian climate, match the site's sun, soil, water, wind, and maintenance realities, and support the landscape concept without blocking the house, lighting, access, or views.

## Use This Skill For

- Trees, shrubs, grasses, perennials, groundcovers, hedges, meadow mixes, edible planting, privacy planting, and seasonal interest.
- Frost-resistant, drought-tolerant, low-maintenance, sandy or sandy-loam palettes.
- Plant schedules, avoid lists, establishment care, mature-size checks, and planting combinations.

Use this skill after `sun-position-analysis` and `soil-irrigation-drainage` when exposure or water conditions are uncertain.

## Inputs Needed

- Region or nearest city in Ukraine.
- Planting zones and roles from `landscape-master-planner`: privacy, structure, meadow, accent, screening, erosion control, softening facade, terrace comfort, or path edge.
- Sun exposure by zone: full sun, partial shade, shade, hot south/west exposure, reflected heat, or wind-exposed.
- Soil type, pH if known, drainage, compaction, irrigation/watering plan, and mulch/soil improvement assumptions.
- Maintenance level, desired style, mature height limits, views to preserve, pets/children concerns, allergy concerns, and winter appearance goals.
- Lighting fixture locations, beams, cable routes, AC service zones, windows, doors, paths, and access constraints.
- Budget and planting size preference: plugs, small containers, larger specimens, bare-root hedging, or phased planting.

If soil is unknown, mark sandy or sandy-loam as an assumption, not a fact.

## Outputs to Produce

- Site and soil assumptions.
- Planting strategy by zone.
- Plant palette table with common name, Latin name, role, mature height/spread, sun, water, soil, frost tolerance, maintenance, and notes.
- Water grouping and irrigation compatibility notes.
- Combination notes: masses, rhythm, seasonal interest, texture, winter structure, and spacing logic.
- Avoid list for unsuitable, invasive, tender, thirsty, brittle, allergenic, or maintenance-heavy plants.
- First-two-season care plan.
- Coordination notes for landscape, lighting, drainage, irrigation, cost, and General Contractor sequencing.

## Coordination Requirements

- Coordinate with `sun-position-analysis` before assigning full-sun, shade, heat-sensitive, or privacy-screen plants.
- Coordinate with `soil-irrigation-drainage` before finalizing plants, because soil texture, drainage, irrigation, and establishment watering affect survival.
- Coordinate with `landscape-master-planner` for plant roles, massing, mature size, spacing, paths, views, and outdoor-room structure.
- Coordinate with `house-lighting-designer` so mature plants do not block facade lighting, path lights, garden lights, junction boxes, transformers, or maintenance access.
- Coordinate with `architecture-review` when plants are close to walls, plinths, windows, doors, AC, downspouts, gutters, or foundations.
- Coordinate with `cost-estimator` after plant sizes, quantities, soil amendments, mulch, irrigation, and labor assumptions are defined.

## Planting Rules

- Prioritize plants suitable for Ukrainian winters and the specific microclimate.
- Match plants to sun, shade, heat, wind, snow load, soil, drainage, water availability, and maintenance level.
- Do not recommend full-sun plants for shaded zones or moisture-loving plants for dry sandy zones without irrigation and soil-improvement assumptions.
- For sandy/sandy-loam soil, favor drought-tolerant plants or state required soil improvement and establishment watering.
- Avoid tender Mediterranean plants as reliable perennials unless winter protection is explicit.
- Use Latin names where possible to reduce nursery ambiguity.
- Check mature size against windows, doors, AC, paths, lighting, views, and maintenance access.
- Prefer resilient masses and repeatable palettes over scattered novelty plants.

## Response Checklist

- Region and hardiness are considered.
- Sun exposure is known or marked as missing/scenario-based.
- Soil, drainage, and irrigation are known, requested, or explicitly assumed.
- Each plant has a role and water group.
- Mature size is checked against house, paths, lights, AC, and views.
- Maintenance needs are realistic.
- Unsuitable plants are named in an avoid list when helpful.
- First-two-season care is included for real planting plans.
