# Промти по ракурсах для ChatGPT-візуалізації

Дата серії: 2026-06-21

Цей файл призначений для окремого прогону кожного ракурсу в ChatGPT / image generation. Для кожного виду потрібно додати відповідне фото з папки `00_Annotated_Viewpoints` або найближчий raw-файл із зони.

## Загальний стиль для всіх ракурсів

```text
Use the uploaded reference photo as the base for a photorealistic evening architectural visualization. Preserve the real one-story Ukrainian countryside house, its roof geometry, wall positions, windows, doors, openings, proportions, column rhythm, and perspective of the uploaded view.

Design direction: organic modern / warm natural minimalism. The house must feel calm, natural, restrained and premium, not high-tech, not classic, not a glossy luxury showroom.

Materials:
- main facade: warm greige / light sand / milky mineral plaster, matte, not pure white;
- roof, gutters, beams, columns and metal elements: dark graphite-brown matte;
- wood: natural warm matte wood, thermowood / larch / plank, not orange glossy varnish;
- floors and steps: warm grey stone, microcement, brushed concrete or outdoor porcelain;
- landscape: gravel strips, ornamental grasses, lavender/sage, low shrubs, natural planting.

Lighting:
- all light is warm white, maximum 2700K;
- use soft dimmed architectural lighting, not bright LED decoration;
- no cold white light, no RGB, no blue light, no yellow neon, no LED outline around the entire roof;
- keep lighting layered, low-glare and realistic for blue hour.

Clean-up:
Remove visual clutter, temporary wires, tools, toys, random buckets, laundry, unfinished construction mess and distracting objects unless they are part of the real architecture.
```
